Overview

This policy applies to the Lumira mobile app. It describes the personal data we collect through the app, why we collect it, how long we retain it, and the choices you have — including exporting or permanently deleting your data.

We only collect what Lumira needs to work: your account details, which apps you've chosen to track, how long they're used, and the scores and coaching messages we generate from that. We do not read your messages, see your screen, or track your location.

What we collect

Account information

  • Email address — from Google Sign-In, used to identify your account and contact you.
  • Display name — from your Google profile, editable by you, shown in the app.
  • Profile photo — the image URL from your Google profile, used as your avatar. We don't store a copy on our servers.

App usage data

  • Tracked apps — the package names of apps you choose to track (e.g. a social media app).
  • Foreground time — how long each tracked app stays open, measured through Android's system usage stats.
  • Session timestamps — when a tracked usage session starts and ends.

Brain state data

  • Brain score — a number we calculate from your usage data.
  • Brain state — a category (Healthy, Stressed, Cracked, Destroyed) based on that score.
  • Snapshot history — your past brain states, so we can show your trend over time.

Coach messages

  • Message content — generated from templates based on your usage, not written by you.
  • Engagement — whether you opened a message and acted on it.

Focus sessions

  • Session records — duration, completion status, and which apps were blocked.

Notifications

  • Device token — used to deliver push notifications, refreshed automatically.
  • Notification log — which notifications were sent and whether you opened them.

Technical data

  • Crash reports — stack traces, device model, and OS version, via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Analytics events — feature usage events, via Firebase Analytics, used to improve app stability and performance.

What we never collect

We designed Lumira so it never needs the following, and we don't collect it:

Screen content or recordings Keystrokes or typed text Messages or notification content Location data Contacts Photos or media files Payment information Health data

Your rights and controls

Export your data

Download everything we hold about you — usage sessions, brain states, coach messages, focus sessions, and settings — as a JSON file from Settings → Privacy → Export Data. The file is generated on demand and saved to your device.

Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently removes your data, generally within 24 hours. You can start this from Settings → Privacy → Delete Account, or from the page below.

Delete my account

Correct your data

You can edit your display name, and change your tracked apps or goals, at any time in Settings.

GDPR (European users)

  • Legal basis: we process usage data under legitimate interest, to provide the core features you've signed up for. Analytics data is processed to improve app performance, stability, and experience.
  • Data minimization: we only collect what each feature actually needs.
  • Right to erasure: available any time via account deletion.
  • Right to data portability: available any time via data export.
  • Data processing agreement: our use of Firebase is covered under Google's Data Processing Agreement.

CCPA (California users)

  • Right to know: all your data is visible inside the app.
  • Right to delete: available any time via account deletion.
  • Right to opt out of sale: not applicable — we do not sell personal information.
  • Non-discrimination: your experience is the same whichever privacy choices you make.

Children's privacy

Lumira is not designed for, or directed at, children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We don't knowingly collect data from children, and no COPPA-specific measures apply since the app is intended for adult users.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we handle your data, we'll update the effective date above and notify you in the app before the changes take effect.
