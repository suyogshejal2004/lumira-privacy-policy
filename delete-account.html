Delete Your Lumira Account

Your privacy is important to us. You may request permanent deletion of your Lumira account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like to permanently delete your Lumira account, please send an email from the email address associated with your Lumira account.

Request Account Deletion

Email Details

  • Email: aquaalert.app@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete My Lumira Account
  • Include the Google account email used to sign in to Lumira.

What Happens After Your Request?

After verifying your request, we will permanently delete your Lumira account and associated personal information from our systems.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your Lumira account
  • User profile information
  • Focus sessions and productivity history
  • Preferences and settings
  • Cloud data associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required by applicable law, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our Terms of Service.

Processing Time:

Most deletion requests are completed within 7 business days. In some cases, deletion may take up to 30 days.
